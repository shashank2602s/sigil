How Sigil works, and where your content goes

No external AI service

Sigil does not send your content to any external AI or third-party API. Every check runs on this server, inside the application. This is a deliberate choice for confidential brand material.

What actually produces a finding

  • Rules: terminology, banned and retired terms, off-brand tone words, exclamation and all-caps limits, sentence length, prohibited promises, required disclaimers, and basic accessibility checks (link text, image alt text).
  • Retrieval: a TF-IDF similarity search (scikit-learn, running locally) matches each sentence that contains a figure to the closest approved fact, then compares the numbers. It also flags facts that have expired.
  • Citation validator: every finding must cite a guideline, approved fact or terminology entry, and the quoted text must exist in that source. Findings that fail this check are hidden and counted.
  • Intent check: suggested edits are compared with the intent you state, and a warning is shown if an edit changes words that relate to it.

These are rule-based and statistical methods, not a language model. They can miss subtle tone or meaning problems, and some flags will be wrong. That is why a person always decides.

Human control

  • Nothing is edited, submitted or approved automatically. Suggested edits are only applied when the creator accepts them and presses the apply button.
  • Blockers that the creator rejects or disputes must be waived or upheld by a reviewer, with a written reason, before approval.
  • Claims and facts are always routed to a human reviewer, and low-confidence findings are labelled.
  • Every decision is recorded in the audit log with who, when and why.

Data and demo notice

This is a hackathon demo. The brand pack, people and content are fictional samples. Data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server and may be reset at any time. Do not enter real confidential information. Access is by demo role only; there is no real authentication in this build.

Development disclosure: this project was built with AI coding assistance (Claude). Libraries used: Flask, scikit-learn, python-docx, pdfplumber, Jinja2.
